Privacy Policy

NomadZohaib Autopost · Effective 10 November 2026

NomadZohaib Autopost ("the App") is a private, single-user tool operated by Nomad Zohaib, a travel content creator. It is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own short videos to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign up or use it.

1. What information the App accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, TikTok provides the App with:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): account name and avatar, used only to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload). The owner reviews and publishes each draft manually in the TikTok app.
  • Basic statistics of the owner's own videos (video.list): view, like, comment and share counts, used only to compare how the owner's own videos perform.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to upload the owner's own videos and to produce a private performance report for the owner. It is never used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's personal computer. There is no server, database, or cloud storage operated by the App. Tokens can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Security & permissions → Apps & websites).

4. Sharing

No information is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

5. Other people's data

The App does not access, collect, or store any data about other TikTok users.

6. Data deletion

The owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the App's local files and revoking access in TikTok settings.
