Bali Day 5: Kuta, Labubu Village, Kuta Beach & a FINNS Sunset

2026-11-10 · 6 min read · by Nomad Zohaib

Day 5 in Bali started with rain and ended with a storm alert at 2 AM. In between: a hotel switch, a whole village of Labubu, Rp 50,000 coconuts and the best sunset of the trip. Here is the whole day, with the tips we wish we knew before.

Morning: checkout in the rain

After five nights at Sunday Hotel in Kuta we checked out under light rain and moved to B Hotel & Spa, just about 200 metres away. Two nights there, then a flight to Jakarta.

NOMAD TIPNovember is rainy season in Bali. Keep the morning flexible, pack a raincoat and a dry bag for your phone.

Afternoon: Beachwalk and the Labubu village

Beachwalk Kuta is across from the beach. Parking was Rp 5,000. Inside, Pop Mart had built a full Labubu Christmas village: every shelf, every corner, Labubu. We also spotted brand sales up to 50% off and Starbucks' Bali city tumbler, a great souvenir.

Kuta Beach: coconuts and 'Oh, Pakistan!'

On Kuta Beach a coconut was Rp 50,000, or two for Rp 100,000 (about PKR 1,800). The big one was Rp 100,000. A local asked where we were from. 'Pakistan!' The reply, with a smile: 'Oh, Pakistan!' Then he showed us a special Bali stone.

NOMAD TIPAsk the price first, and ask for a two-for deal. Carry small notes.

Evening: sunset at FINNS, Canggu

From Kuta it is roughly 10 km to FINNS in Canggu, fastest by scooter. Get there an hour before sunset for a good spot. Stay ten minutes after the sun goes down: that is when the sky really lights up.

2 AM: storm ride home

Late at night the wind picked up, it started to rain, and our phones showed a storm forecast for the next day. We rode back slowly. Check the forecast before late rides.
