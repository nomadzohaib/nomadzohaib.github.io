Kuta to Canggu in One Day: 5-Stop Bali Itinerary

2026-11-10 · 5 min read · by Nomad Zohaib

Only one day between Kuta and Canggu? This is exactly the route we did on Day 5. Save it.

Stop 1 · Base in Kuta (B Hotel & Spa)

Kuta is central: the beach and Beachwalk mall are walkable, and Canggu is roughly 10 km away.

Stop 2 · Beachwalk Kuta

Pop Mart's Labubu Christmas village, shopping and coffee. Parking was Rp 5,000. Perfect if it rains.

Stop 3 · Kuta Beach

Grab a coconut (Rp 50,000, two for 100k) and say hi to the locals.

Stop 4 · FINNS, Canggu for sunset

Scooter about 10 km to Canggu. Aim to arrive an hour before sunset; in November that is around 6:15 PM.

Stop 5 · Canggu at night

Walk the neon streets, then ride back to Kuta. If it is raining, ride slowly.

Scooter rules

  • Helmet, always.
  • Download Google Maps offline.
  • Carry an International Driving Permit.
  • Slow down at night and in rain.
  • Keep small notes for parking.
