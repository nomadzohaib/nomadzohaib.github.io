Bali Prices 2026: What We Really Paid in Kuta (with PKR)

2026-11-10 · 4 min read · by Nomad Zohaib

Everyone asks: is Bali expensive? These are the prices we actually paid on Day 5, not guesses. Conversions use what we paid on the day: Rp 100,000 was about PKR 1,800.

Money tips that saved us

  • Rp 100,000 is '1 lakh'. Count the zeros twice before paying.
  • Carry small notes (Rp 5,000 and 10,000) for parking and snacks.
  • Always ask the price before you order, especially on the beach.
  • Ask for deals: one coconut was 50k, two were 100k.
  • Malls take cards; beach vendors want cash. Carry both.
  • Season sales are real: we saw brands up to 50% off at Beachwalk.

Quick rupee math

Drop three zeros from the Rupiah price and multiply by 18 for a rough PKR figure. Rp 50,000 → 50 × 18 ≈ PKR 900. Rates change, so check the current rate before you travel.
